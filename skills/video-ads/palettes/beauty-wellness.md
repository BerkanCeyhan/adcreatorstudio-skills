# Beauty / Wellness

Skincare, supplements, personal care, mental wellness. Female-skewed or gender-neutral.

## Overlay Colors

| Role | Hex | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Background | `#FAF8F5` or `#1A1512` | Light for day/clean aesthetic; dark for premium |
| Primary text | `#1A1512` or `#F5F0EA` | Contrast on background |
| Accent warm | `#C4875A` | Buttons, highlights, CTA pulse |
| Accent blush | `#E8B4A0` | Softer accent, social proof overlays |
| Neutral mid | `#8B7B6E` | Secondary text, labels |

## Block Style Suggestions

- `dr/hook-bigtext-pop`: Light bg (#FAF8F5), dark text, accent warm kicker
- `hf/instagram-follow`: Dark variant (#1A1512), blush accent border
- `dr/cta-button-pulse`: Warm accent (#C4875A) button, light text
- `dr/solution-product-reveal`: Clean white card, warm accent bullets

## Caption Style
`pop` — bold word-by-word, warm accent highlight

## Vibe
Clean, soft, editorial. Not clinical. Not loud. The texture of a luxury brand, the warmth of a recommendation from a friend.
