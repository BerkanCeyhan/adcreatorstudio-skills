# Finance / Coaching

High-ticket programs, financial services, business coaching. Any gender, 30–55.

## Overlay Colors

| Role | Hex | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Background | `#0A0E1A` | Deep navy-black, premium |
| Primary text | `#F0EBE1` | Warm off-white |
| Accent gold | `#C9A84C` | Premium highlight, CTA |
| Accent light gold | `#E8D5A3` | Secondary accents, labels |
| Structural | `#1E2A3A` | Card backgrounds, subtle panels |

## Block Style Suggestions

- `dr/hook-bigtext-pop`: Navy bg, warm white headline, gold kicker
- `dr/instagram-comment`: Navy card, gold accent line at top
- `dr/cta-button-pulse`: Gold button (#C9A84C), dark text
- `dr/receipt-breakdown`: Dark card, gold totals, warm white labels
- `dr/animated-bullet-list`: Navy card, gold accent bullets

## Caption Style
`classic` — clean, readable, no heavy styling

## Vibe
Premium, authoritative, restrained. The aesthetic of a Bloomberg terminal and a private banking brochure. Gold signals value. Navy signals trust. Nothing is loud.
